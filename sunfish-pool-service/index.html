What the company does

Sunfish Pool Service takes care of backyard pools on a weekly schedule. A visit covers testing the water, skimming, brushing, emptying the baskets and checking the equipment. Plans are flat monthly rates, listed on sunfishswim.com.

The company also does pool repair, quoted in writing before the work starts, and green to clean recoveries for pools that have turned.

How it works

Each pool has a set day. The customer gets a text before the visit, and a photo and report after it. The same crew Douglas Muraki hires and trains does the work.

The company keeps its routes tight on purpose, which is why the service area is a short list. A tech who spends the day at pools and not on the freeway can keep the same day every week.

What it stands on

The mission is serving people through exceptional pool service. Muraki puts it on three things.

Reliability

Same day every week, on time.

Communication

If something is wrong, the customer hears it from Sunfish first, along with the truth about what the equipment needs.

Integrity

Do the job right when nobody is watching, and leave the backyard better than it was found.
