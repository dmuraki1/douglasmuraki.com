Background

Douglas Muraki is a Sacramento business owner. He owns Sunfish Pool Service and runs it full time, after twenty years in service work.

He describes the company this way: the job is serving people, and pool care is how Sunfish does it. The company's mission is serving people through exceptional pool service, and it rests on three things: reliability, communication and integrity.

How he runs the company

Muraki runs the company, not the routes. Sunfish techs service the pools. His part is hiring them, training them, and making sure they have the equipment and support to show up on the same day every week and keep the water right.

He puts real time into teaching. His stated goal is to build the crew up so that any tech who joins could grow into running the company someday.

Customers get a text before the visit and a photo and report after it. If something is wrong with a pool or its equipment, the customer hears it from Sunfish first, and repair work is quoted in writing before it starts.

Outside of work

He fishes and hunts, and keeps a journal of those trips at Cast and Blast Journals. He and his wife plan their travel around food. The photos are on this site, and he writes about the trips on Medium.
